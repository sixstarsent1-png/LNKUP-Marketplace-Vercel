# Phase 18 RFC — Vendor Reliability and Readiness Automation

## Why this phase

Vendor event-day failures are usually readiness/document issues caught too late. This phase emphasizes proactive reliability signals and readiness automation.

## Scope

- Stronger readiness scoring and expiry nudges in vendor surfaces.
- Event-day reliability checklist upgrades in operations/pass views.
- Better pre-event escalation messaging for required documents.

## Out of scope

- No policy changes to verification authority in WordPress.
- No custom push infrastructure beyond current platform constraints.

## Key deliverables

- Readiness risk buckets (green/amber/red) tied to next actions.
- Expiring-doc timeline cards with clear urgency windows.
- Organizer visibility into vendor readiness blockers before event day.

## Success metrics

- Higher document/readiness completion before event date.
- Lower day-of support incidents caused by missing requirements.
- Higher pass-readiness confidence on paid bookings.
