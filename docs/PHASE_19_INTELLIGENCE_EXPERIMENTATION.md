# Phase 19 RFC — Intelligence and Experimentation Layer

## Why this phase

After operational and CRM depth improvements, the next leverage is measurable optimization: better discovery, better conversion, better retention.

## Scope

- Structured experiment framework for marketplace surfaces.
- Funnel diagnostics by role, city, and category segments.
- Operator-facing recommendations tied to observed drop-off points.

## Out of scope

- No invasive personalization requiring new PII classes.
- No replacement of WordPress analytics source of truth.

## Key deliverables

- Experiment registry with rollout metadata and guardrails.
- Segmentable funnel dashboard with alert thresholds.
- Weekly optimization report payload from existing analytics telemetry.

## Success metrics

- Increased search-to-apply conversion.
- Reduced apply-to-paid drop-off.
- Faster experiment cycle time from hypothesis to decision.
